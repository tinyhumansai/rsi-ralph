Grade the change to `pi.py` on how well it justifies its method.

- Does the module or function docstring name the method (series, product or
  formula) and say why it converges faster than the one it replaces?
- Is the number of terms or iterations explained rather than a bare constant?
- Is the code short, standard-library only and free of dead code?

A change that only raises a constant, with no explanation, grades low.
